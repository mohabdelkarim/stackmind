# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment free experience for everyone.

## Our standards

- Be respectful and constructive
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project
- No insults, harassment, or personal attacks

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer via GitHub issues or the contact email on the maintainer profile. The maintainer will review and take appropriate action.
